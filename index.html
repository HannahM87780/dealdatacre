§ 01

The Problem

Bad deals don't announce themselves. They look fine until they don't.

Listing platforms surface inventory. Generic tools surface charts. Neither tells you whether the deal is worth the money — or who else might be a better buyer for it. Most operators end up paying for three subscriptions and still doing the real work in a spreadsheet at midnight.

01

The listing sites are a starting line.

Asking price, square footage, broker name. Useful for finding deals — useless for judging them. Comps, ownership, and risk flags live elsewhere, in five different places.

02

SaaS dashboards bury the answer.

Per-seat pricing, training videos, and analytics you'll never use. By the time you've configured your filters, the deal has gone under contract to somebody who already knew the seller.

03

Generic AI sounds confident, gets it wrong.

Chatbots will happily fabricate comps, ownership chains, and submarket trends. On a $2M deal, "confidently wrong" costs more than the report does.
